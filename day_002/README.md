Then the Tip Calculator. I got stuck for a while on the curly braces, I had forgotten that they only work with `f` in front of the quotes, and then I dropped `:.2f` and lost the two decimal places without noticing. I knew both of these, I just needed a reminder.

Working with `input()` went fine. I did have a doubt about whether the bill should be `int` or `float`. Instead of guessing I changed it to `int` and typed `50.75`, and got `ValueError: invalid literal for int() with base 10: '50.75'`. That settled it. I want to keep doing this, check instead of assume, because guessing is the habit I am trying to break.
